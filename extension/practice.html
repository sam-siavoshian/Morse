Why the best tools disappear

By Ana Ruiz · 4 min read

The tools we love most are the ones we stop noticing. A good pen doesn't ask for attention; it just leaves a line where you meant to put one.

Software is rarely like that. Every app arrives with someone else's idea of where the buttons go, which parts matter and how small the text should be. Most of the time we adjust ourselves to it instead of the other way round.

That is starting to change. When you can point at part of a page and say what you want different, the page can finally fit the person using it.
