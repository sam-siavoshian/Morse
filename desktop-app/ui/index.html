        Connect Chrome

        1. 1
          Add Morse to ChromeMorse opens Chrome's extensions page and shows you its folder. Until Morse is in the Chrome Web Store, you add it from that folder.
          1. In Chrome, turn on Developer mode, at the top right.
          2. Drag the chrome-extension folder Morse showed you onto that page. Or click Load unpacked and choose it: its place is copied, so press ⌘⇧G and paste it.paste it where the window asks for a folder.

        2. 2
          Allow it hereOn the welcome page Chrome opens, click Connect. Morse asks whether Chrome may use your model. Click Allow. There's no code to type.
        3. 3
          Or type a codeIn Chrome, click Morse in the toolbar. Type this code under Morse helper.

        Mods

        A mod is a change you saved. Morse brings it back every time you open that app.

        All mods are off. They are saved, and come back when you turn them on.
