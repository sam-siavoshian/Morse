Your apps

Open an app with Morse, then press in it. Point at something and say what to change.

Choose a model first. Morse needs one to write your changes.

    Morse opens the app again with one extra setting so it can connect. It never changes the app's files. Disconnect, or quit the app, and your mods come off until next time.
