Summer reading starts June 3

Posted May 20 by Dana Ortiz, children's librarian · 4 minute read

Every summer, thousands of children in Riverside read their way through the long weeks between school years. This year the program starts on June 3 and runs until the end of August. Anyone under eighteen can join, and so can grown-ups who want to keep a reading log of their own. Sign up at any branch, or online with your library card.

Each book you finish earns a stamp in your reading passport. Ten stamps get you a free book to keep, chosen from our summer shelf. Twenty stamps put your name on the wall of readers in the children's room. There is no limit to the number of stamps, and audiobooks, comics and picture books all count. Read the full rules before you start.

Last summer more than four thousand readers took part, and visits to the library kept growing all spring. We expect this to be the busiest summer yet, so the reading room will stay open late on weekdays.

Story time moves outside to the garden when the weather is good. Bring a blanket and a snack. On rainy days we meet in the reading corner on the second floor, next to the picture books.
