Morse launch plan

Created Oct 14 · Last edited by Sam

The goal for launch week is simple: get twenty people to make a change to an app they use every day, and record what they asked for.

Before launch

Finish the share card, make the first-run flow shorter, and write the README section about bringing your own model. Test on at least three real Electron apps.

Launch day

Post the clip of the sleep timer, answer every comment in the first two hours, and keep a list of every request that failed so we can turn them into evals.

After

Read every failed request. Group them. Fix the biggest group first.
