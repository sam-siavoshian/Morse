Change any app

Press ⌥ Space, point at something, and say what you want. Morse makes the change while you watch, and keeps it as a mod you can share with a link.

Works in websites and Electron desktop apps. Uses Claude Code, Codex, or your own API key.

How it works

Point. Say it. Watch it change.

  1. ⌥Space
    01

    Point at anything

    Press ⌥ Space in any page or app and click a button, a sidebar or a feed. Or skip pointing and describe a change to the whole page.

  2. “hide Shorts”
    02

    Say what you want

    One sentence. Restyle it, hide it, or add something that wasn’t there: a word count, a sleep timer, a focus mode.

  3. 03

    Watch it change

    The new look shows up while the model is still writing. If the new code fails, Morse shows the model what went wrong and it fixes it. Save it, undo it, or edit the code.

Edit tools

Voice

Hold the shortcut and talk.

Tap ⌥ Space to type. Hold it to speak. Let go, and Morse starts on the change.

  • In Chrome, the browser turns your speech into text.
  • In desktop apps, Morse uses your OpenAI key or Whisper on your computer.
  • Shortcuts are yours to change. Morse refuses the ones that would quit your app.
release to send

Models

Bring your own model.

Morse has no server and no account. It hands what you pointed at and what you said to the coding model you already use, and runs what comes back.

  • Claude Code Uses your existing Claude Code sign-in.
  • Codex Uses your existing Codex CLI sign-in.
  • An API key Anthropic or OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible server in the cloud or on your computer (llama.cpp, LM Studio, vLLM).

Where it works

Websites and desktop apps.

Most apps you use every day are built from web pages. Morse adds one small layer inside them, in the browser or in the app.

Any website

Privacy

        
Your mods stay on your computer.

        
There is no Morse account and no Morse server. Nothing you make is uploaded unless you share it.

      

      

        

          
Stored locally

          
Desktop mods live in ~/.morse/mods. Website mods live in the extension’s own storage.

        

        

          
Links carry the mod

          
A share link holds the whole mod after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server.

        

        

          
You see the code

          
Every change shows its code, can be undone, and is never applied silently. Shared mods show every line before install.

        

        

          
You pick the model

          
What you pointed at, a short outline of the page and your sentence go to the model you chose. Nothing else.
