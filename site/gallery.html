Example mods

A mod is a change you saved. Morse brings it back every time you open that site or app. Here are small mods for apps you already use. Open one to see what it does. Then install it in Morse, or download it for Tampermonkey.

Morse made most of these from the request shown. Each was tested on the real page in October 2026, and again after a reload. Mods marked “Demo app” are for the Morse demo apps. Sites change, so a mod may stop working. If one does, open Morse on that page and say “fix this”.
