Example mods

A mod is a change you saved. Morse brings it back every time you open that site or app. These are small ones for apps you already use. See what each one does, then install it in Morse or download it for Tampermonkey.

Most of these were made by Morse from the request you see, then checked on the real page in October 2026: they work, and still work after a reload. The ones marked “Demo app” are for the Morse demo apps. Apps change often, so a mod may stop working. If one does, open Morse in that app and say “fix this”.
