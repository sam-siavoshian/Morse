Example mods.

A mod is a change to an app, saved with Morse. These are small ones for apps you already use. See what each one does, then install it in Morse or download it for Tampermonkey.

Written by hand for each app in October 2026. Apps change often, so a mod may stop working. If one does, open Morse in that app and say “fix this”.

No mods match that.

Morse can make it for you: press ⌥ Space in the app and say what you want.
