# Morse launch kit

The landing page shows the Morse bar in a real app. It does not play a film.

The launch films, posters and GIFs were 100,464,140 bytes: several cuts of one recording (16:9, 4:5 and 9:16, with and without music), a muted loop, and short clips. They are not in this tree. The shot list for the 33 second cut is in [opus-open-CUT.md](opus-open-CUT.md). The music credit is in [MUSIC.md](MUSIC.md). The trace for the how-it-works recording is in [how-it-works-trace.log](how-it-works-trace.log).
