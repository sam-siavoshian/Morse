## How the edit uses it

The usual shape of a tech clip that travels: an upbeat electronic track around
120 BPM, a riser into a drop on the first reveal, hard cuts on the beat, and a
stop before the end card.

- The video starts 1.48 s before the drop at 1:19.17 in the track, in the middle
  of its riser (the sweep from 1:17.5). The drop lands on the first change, VS
  Code's new look.
- Every cut is on a beat (122 BPM: a beat every 0.49 s). Each scene is a whole
  number of beats long.
- One beat of silence before the end card; the end card comes in on a downbeat
  and the music fades out under it.
- Mixed to about -14 LUFS, a common level for social video.

## Without music

`morse-v3-16x9-nomusic.mp4`, `morse-v3-4x5-nomusic.mp4` and
`morse-v3-9x16-nomusic.mp4` are the same cuts with no sound. Use them to add a
trending sound inside X, TikTok or Instagram: their in-app libraries are
licensed for use on that platform. Pick a sound around 120 BPM with a drop near
the start, and line the drop up with the first change (1.5 s in).
